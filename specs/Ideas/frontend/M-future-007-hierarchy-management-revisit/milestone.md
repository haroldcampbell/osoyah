# M-future-007-Hierarchy Management Revisit: Tree Reorder UX

Conform to `docs/principles.md`.

## Summary
Plan a future milestone to revisit hierarchy management so tree reordering feels direct, reliable, and visible.

## Goal
Provide stable drag-and-drop reordering in the hierarchy tree without hidden side effects or drop failures.

## Scope
In-scope:
- Drag-and-drop reordering directly in the hierarchy tree.
- Clear placeholder and drag affordances that match list behavior.
- Reliable drop registration across nested levels.
Out-of-scope:
- New hierarchy entities or persistence.
- Bulk editing or cross-tree moves.
